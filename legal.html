Privacy notice

Who is responsible. The operator named above. Because the study is reachable from the European Economic Area, this notice is written to satisfy the General Data Protection Regulation as well as United States law; where the GDPR applies, the operator is the controller within the meaning of Article 4(7).

What the study is, technically. A set of static files: a page, a script, a stylesheet, the card deck as a data file, and images. It runs entirely in your browser. There is no server-side program, no account, no login, no form, and no score is ever sent anywhere; the benchmark against bot games is computed on your own device. The study sets no cookies, uses no analytics or advertising service, calls no artificial-intelligence service, and loads nothing from third-party servers: fonts are those installed on your device, images and data come from this site itself.

One entry on your device. So that you can continue where you stopped, the study keeps your mode, your decisions and a random seed in your browser's local storage, under the name ironhammer_state. Nothing else is stored. The entry contains no personal information and is never sent anywhere; it persists until you clear this site's data or start again. If your browser blocks local storage, the study still works but forgets your decisions when the tab is closed.

Server logs. The study is hosted by Netlify, whose infrastructure records the requests it serves, typically the IP address, time, the address requested, the status, the amount of data transferred, and the browser's user-agent and referrer strings. This is technically unavoidable in delivering a website and is the only collection of data about players. It is used to operate and secure the service, not to build profiles, and the operator does not analyse it. Where the GDPR applies, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f); retention follows Netlify's own periods. For players in the European Economic Area this means their request data are processed in the United States, by the operator and by Netlify Inc.
